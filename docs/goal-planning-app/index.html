Goal planning app

Plan goals beyond the finish line.

A useful goal planner does more than preserve ambition. It makes the next action visible, protects repeatable support, and helps the plan change when reality changes.

Download Loom on the App Store Read practical guides

A goal planning app should help answer three questions: What change am I trying to create? What finite work moves it forward? What repeatable behavior helps the progress survive ordinary life? Loom connects those answers inside one iPhone system.

What to look for in a goal planner

Choose a planner that keeps the outcome visible without hiding the next action. It should distinguish between Goals, finite work, and repeatable behavior; preserve why the Goal matters; and make review easier than abandoning the plan and starting over.

Goals need whole-life context

A career Goal can be successful and still create avoidable damage if health, relationships, or money become invisible. Loom connects Goals to Fulfillment Areas so users can see the part of life each Goal is intended to strengthen.

Action Plans make progress observable

Action Plans hold the finite work: submit the form, schedule the meeting, draft the page, complete the first training session, or review the numbers. Dates, duration, Musts, people, places, attachments, notes, and status add context only where it helps execution.

Little Wins protect consistency

Many Goals need repeatable support. A Goal to write a manuscript may use a daily ten-minute writing Little Win. A financial Goal may use a Friday spending review. A health Goal may use selected-day movement with optional permitted Apple Health signals.

Example: complete a professional certification

The Goal belongs to Career & Business and includes the target exam date. Action Plans include enroll, schedule study blocks, complete each practice exam, and submit required documentation. The current Must is registering for the exam. A Little Win supports twenty minutes of study on selected weekdays.

Reflection shows whether the schedule is realistic and whether the next Action Plan should change.

How LoomAI can help

LoomAI can help clarify a Goal, suggest next actions, rewrite vague language, or propose Little Wins using the user's Loom context. Suggestions remain optional and should be reviewed before becoming commitments.

When a dedicated project tool may be better

Large teams, complex dependencies, shared files, and formal delivery workflows may require team project-management software. Loom is designed for the individual's life-management context rather than enterprise project administration.

Current Loom capabilities

FAQ

Common questions

What makes Loom a goal planning app?
Loom connects Goals to Fulfillment Areas, Action Plans, Musts, Little Wins, Capture, Reflection, and LoomAI support.
Can I track habits beside goals?
Yes. Little Wins provide repeatable actions inside the same life-management structure as Goals and Action Plans.
Can LoomAI create next steps?
LoomAI can suggest and draft next steps using Loom context. The user reviews and chooses what becomes part of the plan.
Is Loom available on iPhone?
Yes. Loom is built for iPhone and is available through the App Store.
