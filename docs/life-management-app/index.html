Life management app

A life management app for the whole picture.

Loom gives goals, tasks, habits, purpose, and reflection one shared structure so daily action does not drift away from the life it is meant to support.

Download Loom on the App Store Read practical guides

A life management app helps you decide what deserves attention across work and personal life—not merely store more tasks. Loom is built for iPhone users whose responsibilities span goals, routines, relationships, health, money, work, learning, and the incoming details of an ordinary day.

Why separate productivity tools start to feel disconnected

A task manager can show what is due. A habit tracker can show what repeated. A notes app can remember context. A goal app can preserve ambition. Each tool can work well and still leave you responsible for reconstructing how everything relates.

The missing layer is often not another feature. It is a shared structure that connects what matters, what should change, what needs action, what should repeat, and what the last week taught you.

How Loom organizes the whole loop

Purpose

Describe your larger direction and the Passions that reveal what drives you.

Fulfillment Areas

Create 3–7 areas that represent the important parts of your life, including custom areas.

Goals

Define outcomes with timelines, progress, and a relationship to the area they support.

Action Plans

Manage finite work with dates, duration, Musts, people, places, attachments, notes, and status.

Little Wins

Keep a few repeatable daily actions visible where they contribute to meaningful progress.

Capture and Reflection

Collect incoming context, then review what happened and adjust what comes next.

A realistic day inside a life management app

You capture a request from an email, notice that a Health & Energy Little Win is still open, complete the Must that advances a current Goal, and reschedule an action that depends on another person. Later, Reflection helps you recognize what worked and what should change.

The point is not to touch every feature every day. It is to keep the information available inside one model so that the next decision does not require rebuilding your life from several apps.

Example: a demanding launch week

A founder can make “ship the new onboarding experience” a Career & Business Goal, mark the release checklist as the current Must, assign a review action to a collaborator, and keep a short walk and family dinner as Little Wins in other Fulfillment Areas. Capture holds incoming ideas without allowing them to replace the launch plan.

Where LoomAI fits

LoomAI can help clarify Goals, draft Action Plans, suggest Little Wins, rewrite notes, and explore priorities using the context already present in Loom. It supports the system; it is not the system. The user remains responsible for choosing what matters and approving what becomes part of the plan.

Who may prefer something simpler

If you need only a grocery list, a shared work project board, or a calendar for appointments, a specialized app may be a better fit. Loom is strongest when your real problem is keeping several parts of life connected to daily action on iPhone.

Current Loom capabilities

One system from direction to reflection.

Loom is designed for the moment when separate task, habit, goal, notes, and reflection tools create more maintenance than clarity.

  • Purpose and Passions clarify what matters.
  • Custom Fulfillment Areas organize the parts of life you intend to strengthen.
  • Goals, Action Plans, Musts, and Little Wins turn direction into progress.
  • Capture holds incoming commitments until you decide what they mean.
  • Reflection and LoomAI help review, clarify, and adjust the plan.

FAQ

Common questions

What is a life management app?
A life management app helps organize goals, tasks, repeatable actions, life areas, incoming commitments, and review in one connected system.
How is Loom different from a to-do list?
A to-do list primarily stores finite actions. Loom connects Action Plans to Purpose, Fulfillment Areas, Goals, Little Wins, Capture, and Reflection.
Does Loom work on Android or the web?
Loom is currently an iPhone app. Do not choose Loom if Android or web access is a current requirement.
Does Loom use Apple Health?
Apple Health support is optional and permission-based. Supported signals can help ground configured Goals and Little Wins without making health guarantees.
