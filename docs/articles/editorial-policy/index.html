Editorial policy

Independent, useful, and grounded in the real product.

Loom articles explain genuine reader problems, show only current Loom capabilities, and clearly separate Loom's original product from the broader work that inspired its founder.

Last updated August 12, 2026

People first

Each article must answer a real question, explain why the problem persists, offer a practical way to think or act, and disclose where a simpler tool may be enough. Search visibility is a distribution goal, not permission to publish thin or repetitive pages.

Current product evidence

Every Loom capability claim is checked against the current app implementation and an authentic screenshot when the article visually demonstrates it. Roadmap features are excluded until released. Optional Apple Health support is described without medical or guaranteed outcome claims.

Original language and independent design

Loom uses its own product language, structure, examples, and editorial explanations. Private research may inform problem understanding, but public Loom content does not quote, copy, link to, imply affiliation with, or claim to implement any third-party productivity methodology.

Loom-original language

Purpose, Fulfillment Areas, Action Plans, Musts, Little Wins, Capture, Reflection, and “End Stress and Live Fulfilled” are presented as parts of Loom’s own system and founding story.

Commercial disclosure

Spencer Daugherty owns Loom and benefits when readers download or purchase the app. Articles use campaign-tagged App Store links so Loom can measure whether organic content produces useful acquisition. Measurement does not change the editorial standard or the requirement to describe limitations honestly.

Corrections and updates

Substantive updates receive a modified date. Product changes trigger a claim review before old content is reused. Questions or correction requests can be sent to spencer@loomlife.us.
