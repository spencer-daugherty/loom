Founder and author

Spencer Daugherty

Spencer built Loom after years of managing business leadership, ambition, health, relationships, and a never-ending task list that could record activity but could not represent the life behind it.

Spencer is the founder and product builder behind Loom: Life Manager. His work sits at the intersection of company leadership, product strategy, execution, and the practical challenge of keeping several areas of life from becoming disconnected.

Why Loom exists

Spencer's original founding theme is End Stress and Live Fulfilled. It describes the problem that motivated Loom: moving quickly through a growing list while still feeling that health, relationships, meaning, or long-term goals could slip out of view.

Loom's primary positioning—Your life is not a to-do list—grew from the same experience. Loom is designed to connect what matters with what a person actually does through Purpose, Fulfillment Areas, Goals, Action Plans, Little Wins, Capture, and Reflection.

How the articles are written

Articles begin with a real reader problem, use the current Loom product as the source of truth, and include limitations where a simpler or more specialized tool may be a better fit. Broader work on productivity, personal development, goals, habits, and reflection informs Spencer's understanding, but the published language and structure remain independently written for Loom.

Transparency

Spencer owns Loom and benefits when readers download or purchase the app. Product claims are checked against current implementation, and named inspirations are disclosed separately from Loom's independent product model.
