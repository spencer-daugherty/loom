Loom is a to-do list app for iPhone users who need more than task storage. Capture holds incoming commitments, Action Plans organize finite work, Musts identify the actions that matter most, and Goals preserve the reason the work belongs on the list.

Why a useful list needs more than due dates

A conventional list answers “What could I do?” That is useful until work, relationships, health, money, learning, and personal projects all compete for the same attention. Then a longer list can preserve obligations without helping you decide which one should move now.

The answer is not to attach a complex framework to every errand. It is to give important tasks enough context to remain connected to a meaningful outcome while allowing simple tasks to stay simple.

How tasks move through Loom

Capture

Save an incoming thought, link, request, or responsibility before it is lost.

Action Plans

Turn finite work into clear actions with dates, duration, status, people, places, notes, and attachments.

Musts

Mark the small number of actions that deserve priority instead of treating every open item equally.

Goals and life areas

Keep meaningful actions connected to the outcome and Fulfillment Area they support.

Little Wins

Separate repeatable progress from finite tasks so routines do not clutter the action list.

Reflection

Review what happened, clear stale work, and adjust the plan instead of carrying everything forward.

Example: planning a demanding Tuesday

You capture a request from a client, add “send revised scope” to the Action Plan for a current business Goal, and mark it as a Must. A short walk remains a Little Win in Health & Energy rather than another one-off task. A grocery reminder can stay simple. Loom gives the important work context without forcing every item into the same structure.

Where LoomAI helps

LoomAI can help clarify a Goal, draft an Action Plan, suggest Little Wins, rewrite notes, and explore priorities using the context already in Loom. It can propose structure, but you decide what matters and approve what becomes part of the plan.

When a simpler to-do list is enough

If you need only a shared grocery list, a team project board, or the same task list on Android and the web, a specialized tool may be a better fit. Loom is currently an individual iPhone app and is strongest when tasks must stay connected to several goals and parts of life.

Common questions

Is Loom a to-do list app?
Yes. Loom includes Capture and Action Plans for managing tasks, then connects those tasks to Goals, Fulfillment Areas, Little Wins, Purpose, and Reflection.
Who should use Loom instead of a simple list?
Loom is best suited to iPhone users whose tasks span several responsibilities and need to stay connected to goals, repeatable actions, life areas, and review.
Does Loom work on Android or the web?
Loom is currently available for iPhone. It is not currently an Android, web, or team project-management app.
