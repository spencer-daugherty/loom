Streaks alternative for iPhone

Loom vs Streaks: choose the system that matches the job.

Choose Streaks when you want focused habit tracking, Apple Health automation, and Apple Watch support. Choose Loom when repeatable actions need to stay connected to life areas, Goals, Action Plans, daily Musts, Capture, and Reflection.

Updated August 16, 2026 by Loom: Life Manager. This first-party comparison uses the released apps and official publisher sources. Loom is not affiliated with Streaks.

What is the best Streaks alternative?

Loom is the stronger alternative when the missing piece is context, not another streak counter. It connects repeatable Little Wins to Fulfillment Areas, Goals, finite Action Plans, current Musts, Capture, and Reflection. Streaks remains the better fit when you want a focused habit-forming to-do list across Apple devices, including Apple Watch, with up to 24 tasks and automatic completion for supported Apple Health goals.

Neither app is the universal answer. Loom is not available on Android or the web and does not offer an Apple Watch app. Streaks is not positioned as a whole-life planning system.

Choose Loom if…

  • You want repeatable actions tied to Goals and customizable life areas.
  • You want finite Action Plans, current Musts, and Capture beside habits.
  • You want Reflection and optional AI-assisted planning inside the same structure.
  • You plan primarily on iPhone or iPad and do not need Android, web, or Apple Watch access.

Choose Streaks if…

  • You mainly want a focused habit-forming to-do list.
  • You want iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, or Apple Vision Pro support.
  • You want supported Apple Health goals to complete automatically.
  • You prefer a one-time App Store purchase and do not need a broader life-management model.

When Loom becomes the better fit

If Streaks covers the repeated actions but your Goals, finite tasks, incoming notes, and weekly review still live elsewhere, Loom addresses that specific gap. Its differentiator is the visible connection between why something matters, what Goal it supports, what action comes next, and what Little Win should repeat.

Loom is most relevant for iPhone and iPad users planning across multiple life areas who do not need Android, web, Mac, or Apple Watch access.

Realistic Loom workflows

  • Connect a repeatable workout Little Win to a health Goal, then review the wider progress separately from the streak.
  • Use small repeatable actions to support a Goal in work, relationships, learning, or home.
  • Keep the next finite Action Plan and the repeatable Little Win visible in the same planning system.

When a streak is not enough

A streak answers whether a behavior happened repeatedly. It does not by itself decide whether that behavior still supports the right Goal, what finite action should happen next, or what the last week revealed. Loom is designed for that broader planning and review layer.

How to test Loom in one week

Start by adding one meaningful Goal, three finite Action Plans, and two Little Wins. At the end of the week, use Reflection to check whether the work moved the Goal forward. Keep Loom only if that connected loop is more useful for your workflow than a focused habit tracker.

What not to optimize for

Do not choose a productivity app only because it has the longest feature list. The better question is whether its primary workflow matches the job you need it to do with the least maintenance.

Migration path from Streaks to Loom

You do not need to move every old item on day one. Start with active goals, current commitments, and the repeat behaviors you care about most. Keep archived projects where they are, then use Loom for the forward-looking system: what matters now, what action comes next, and what small win should repeat. This avoids turning migration into another productivity project.

For many users, the practical switch is gradual: keep Streaks available while Loom becomes the place for personal goals, habits, and weekly reflection. Once daily planning feels more coherent in Loom, retire the parts of the old workflow that are duplicative.

Pricing and platform fit

At the August 16, 2026 source review, the U.S. App Store lists Streaks at $5.99. Loom is a free download whose continued access requires an in-app purchase, with monthly, annual, and lifetime options. Loom supports iPhone and iPad; Streaks also supports Mac, Apple Watch, and Apple Vision Pro. Storefront pricing and compatibility can change, so check the official listings before deciding.

Common questions

Is Loom a habit tracker alternative to Streaks?
Yes, when you want repeatable actions connected to Goals, Action Plans, daily priorities, Capture, and Reflection. Streaks remains the stronger fit for focused streak tracking across Apple devices.
Does Loom replace every habit app?
No. Loom is best when repeatable progress needs whole-life context. If you mainly want up to 24 habit tasks, Apple Watch access, or automatic completion from supported Apple Health goals, Streaks may be the simpler fit.
Is Loom available for iPhone?
Yes. Loom is released for iPhone and iPad and requires iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. It is not currently available on Android or the web.
Is Loom affiliated with Streaks?
No. Loom is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Streaks or Crunchy Bagel.
