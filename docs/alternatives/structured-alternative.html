Loom vs Structured: which planner fits you?

Compare a visual day timeline with a whole-life system that connects long-term direction to daily action.

Loom is a strong Structured alternative for iPhone when you need whole-life planning rather than a visual day timeline. Choose Structured when time blocking, calendar integration, and cross-device scheduling are central. Choose Loom when daily actions need to connect to customizable Fulfillment Areas, Goals, Projects, Action Plans, Little Wins, Capture, and Reflection.

Loom is limited to iPhone and iPad and is not a direct visual-timeline replacement.

Choose Loom when...

  • You want Purpose and customizable life areas to guide priorities.
  • You want Goals, Projects, finite actions, daily Musts, and repeatable Little Wins connected.
  • You want Capture, Reflection, and optional LoomAI guidance inside the same planning model.
  • You plan primarily on iPhone or iPad and do not need a browser or Android app.

Choose Structured when...

  • You primarily want to time-block tasks and events on a visual daily timeline.
  • Calendar and Reminders imports are central to your day.
  • You want an Inbox, subtasks, routines, a focus timer, and weekly or monthly schedule views.
  • You need availability across Apple devices, Android, or the web, understanding that features vary by platform.

Try Loom for whole-life planning

What does a realistic Loom workflow look like?

Suppose you want to launch a new consulting offer without letting work crowd out the rest of life.

  1. Create a Career & Business Goal with a clear finish line.
  2. Use a Project to organize milestone date ranges and the actions required for the launch.
  3. Make “finish the first offer draft” today’s Must.
  4. Keep a short walk and family dinner as Little Wins in other Fulfillment Areas.
  5. Use Capture for incoming ideas and Reflection to choose what changes next.

See how to turn a Goal into an Action Plan and today’s Must, or compare Goals, habits, and tasks.

When is Loom not a replacement for Structured?

Do not switch to Loom expecting the same visual day timeline, calendar-centered time blocking, Focus Timer, Android app, or browser access. Structured is the clearer fit for those needs.

Loom is also not a shared team-project workspace or a calendar-only planner. Its advantage is the whole-life hierarchy around the work: Purpose, Fulfillment Areas, Goals, Projects, Actions, Little Wins, Capture, and Reflection.

If that hierarchy sounds closer to your problem, compare Loom’s complete life-manager fit and limits.

How do you move from Structured without rebuilding everything?

You do not need to migrate every old item. Start with one active Goal, the current finite work, and one or two repeatable actions you still care about. Keep historical schedules in Structured while you test the forward-looking loop in Loom.

After a week, review whether Loom made it easier to connect the result, the next action, the repeatable support, and the life area receiving attention. If you still need Structured’s visual day timeline, the two apps can serve different jobs; the comparison does not require an all-or-nothing switch.

What do platform and price change?

Loom is a free App Store download for iPhone and iPad, and access requires an in-app purchase. On August 16, 2026, the U.S. listing showed $15 monthly, $79 annually, and $129 lifetime options.

Structured can be used free with optional Pro features. Its official U.S. pricing page showed $6.99 monthly, $29.99 annually, and $99.99 lifetime on the same review date. Storefront pricing and feature availability can change.

Common questions

Is Loom a visual daily planner like Structured?
No. Loom has a Today view and Projects with milestone Gantt timelines, but it is not a direct replacement for Structured’s hourly visual day timeline or calendar-centered time blocking.
Who should choose Loom over Structured?
Choose Loom when the main problem is connecting Purpose, customizable life areas, Goals, Projects, finite actions, Little Wins, Capture, and Reflection—not only scheduling a day.
Which app has broader device access?
Structured supports Apple devices, Android, and the web, with some features varying by platform. Loom is released for iPhone and iPad and does not offer Android or browser access.
Do both apps include AI assistance?
Yes. LoomAI offers contextual planning guidance that the user reviews. Structured Pro offers Structured AI and Replan on supported platforms. AI is not an exclusive differentiator for either app.
Is Loom affiliated with Structured?
No. Loom is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Structured or Apple.
